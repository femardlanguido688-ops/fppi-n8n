Privacy Policy – FPPI n8n

Last updated: November 7, 2026

FPPI n8n is an internal automation tool used only by the Engineering & Maintenance Department of Filipinas Palmoil Plantations, Inc. (FPPI).

What the app accesses

  • Google Sheets: reads and updates the department's own maintenance spreadsheets (work orders, preventive maintenance, lubrication and attendance records).
  • Gmail: sends the department's maintenance status emails from the department mailbox.

How the data is used

The data is used only to run the department's maintenance reports and notifications. It is processed on the department's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Who can use the app

Only FPPI Engineering & Maintenance staff who sign in with their own Google account. Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account settings (Security → Third-party apps with account access).
